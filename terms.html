1. Our Services

Lumiu is a reminders application that enables users to create scheduled push notifications with flexible repeat options. The App is available in two tiers:

  • Free tier: Up to 3 reminders stored locally on your device. No account is required. Reminder data is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Paid tier: Unlimited reminders with cloud sync across devices. Requires account creation and an active subscription. Reminder data is stored in Firebase Firestore.
