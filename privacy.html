1. What Information Do We Collect?

Free tier users

If you use Lumiu's free tier, no account is required and no personal information is collected, transmitted, or stored by us. All reminder data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage and never leaves it.

Paid tier users — information you provide

If you subscribe to Lumiu's paid tier, you will create an account. We collect the following information:

  • Email address (used for authentication via Firebase)
  • Reminder titles, scheduled times, and repeat settings you create
  • Device push notification token (to deliver your reminders)
  • Subscription status and purchase receipt data (managed via RevenueCat)

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personal data beyond what is listed above.

Information automatically collected

We automatically collect certain technical information when you use the Services, such as your IP address, device type, operating system, and usage data. This information is used solely to maintain the security and operation of our Services and for internal analytics. It does not reveal your specific identity.

Sensitive information: we do not process sensitive personal information.

2. How Do We Process Your Information?

We process your personal information for the following reasons:

  • To create and manage your account
  • To sync your reminders across devices (paid tier only)
  • To deliver push notifications at your scheduled times
  • To manage and validate your subscription
  • To respond to your enquiries and provide support
  • To maintain the security and proper functioning of the Services
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

3. When and With Whom Do We Share Your Personal Information?

We do not sell your personal information. We share your information only with the following third-party service providers who are necessary to operate the Services:

Firebase (Google LLC)

We use Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, provided by Google LLC, for user authentication and cloud data storage in the paid tier. Data may be processed in the United States under Google's standard contractual clauses. See Google's Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat, Inc.

Subscription and in-app purchase management is handled by RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat processes your purchase receipt and subscription status. They do not receive your reminder content. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Expo (Expo Technology, Inc.)

Push notifications are delivered via Expo's notification infrastructure, which processes your device push token to route notifications to your device. See Expo's Privacy Policy.

Business transfers

We may share or transfer your information in connection with any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business.

4. Do We Use Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies?

The Lumiu mobile app does not use cookies. This website does not currently use cookies for tracking or analytics purposes. If this changes, we will update this notice and obtain your consent where required.

5. How Do We Handle Push Notifications?

Delivering reminders via push notifications is the core function of Lumiu. To do this, we request permission to send you notifications and collect a device push token — a unique identifier that allows notification services to route a notification to your specific device.

Free tier users: notifications are scheduled entirely on-device using local notifications. No push token is collected or transmitted.

Paid tier users: your push token is stored in Firebase Firestore and shared with Expo's notification service solely for the purpose of delivering your reminders. It is not used for marketing or advertising.

You can withdraw notification permissions at any time in your device settings. This will prevent reminders from being delivered but will not affect your account or data.

6. Is Your Information Transferred Internationally?

Our primary infrastructure is operated via Firebase, with servers located in the European Union where possible. However, some data may be processed in the United States by Google LLC, RevenueCat, and Expo Technology. Where this occurs, we rely on standard contractual clauses and other lawful transfer mechanisms to ensure your data is protected in accordance with GDPR.

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the Services or as required by law. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our active databases. Some information may be retained in backup archives for a limited period before permanent deletion.

Free tier users have no data stored with us. Local data on your device can be cleared by uninstalling the app.
